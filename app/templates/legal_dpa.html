Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Art. 28 GDPR

This agreement applies when {{ settings.company_name }} ("Processor") processes personal data contained in email on behalf of a {{ settings.app_name }} customer ("Controller"). It forms part of the terms of service and is accepted when you create an account.

1. Subject matter and duration

Relaying email between the Controller's domain addresses, the Controller's Gmail mailbox, and the Controller's correspondents, for as long as the Controller's account exists.

2. Nature and purpose

Receiving, rewriting the routing headers of, cryptographically signing, and delivering email messages; keeping minimal routing metadata to enable replies and threading.

3. Data and data subjects

Data subjects: the Controller's correspondents and the Controller's users. Data: email addresses, display names, Message-IDs, delivery timestamps and status, and message content in transit only.

4. Processor obligations

  • Process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, which are: relay mail as configured in the dashboard.
  • Make sure staff with access are bound by confidentiality.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (Art. 32), including encryption in transit, encryption of signing keys at rest, access control, not storing message content, and data minimisation.
  • Engage sub-processors only as listed in the privacy policy, and give 30 days' notice of changes, during which the Controller may object and terminate.
  • Help the Controller respond to data subject requests and meet its Art. 32–36 obligations.
  • Notify the Controller without undue delay, and within 48 hours where possible, after becoming aware of a personal data breach.
  • Delete all personal data at the end of the service (account deletion), except where EU or Member State law requires it to be kept.
  • Make available the information needed to show compliance, and allow and contribute to reasonable audits.

5. International transfers

Processing takes place in the EU. Email addressed to recipients outside the EU is necessarily delivered to their providers at the Controller's instruction.
