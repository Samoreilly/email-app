Questions

What exactly is Google changing?

From January 2027 Gmail removes "Send as" for third-party addresses on the web and in the mobile apps, and stops Gmailify and POP fetching. Forwarding mail into Gmail still works, so receiving is fine, but you can no longer reply as your own domain. Google's announcement: support.google.com/mail/answer/17101213.

Will people see my Gmail address?

No. We remove the headers that would reveal it, replace the Message-IDs Gmail generates, and swap addresses in the quoted text. Recipients only see you@yourdomain.com.

How do I start a brand-new email (not a reply)?

Use the Compose page (it works on your phone), or add a contact in the dashboard. That gives you a relay address to email from Gmail, and you can save it in your phone's contacts.

Do you store my email?

No. Messages pass through in transit and are never written to disk. We keep only routing metadata (who wrote to which of your addresses, and when) to make replies and threading work. See our privacy policy.

Can I use it with @yahoo.com or @outlook.com addresses?

No. Sending as an address properly needs control over that domain's DNS, so {{ settings.app_name }} works with custom domains you own.

What happens if I stop paying?

Replies pause straight away. Incoming mail keeps forwarding for {{ settings.inbound_grace_days }} more days so nothing gets lost while you sort things out.
